Apexo Clinical Beta

Version 0.15.0-beta.1 · colleague feedback build · 4 September 2026

Start safely: extract the ZIP, open Apexo-Windows-Portable/apexo.exe, and choose Demo. Back up PocketBase before testing with real clinic data.

Clinical additions

Therapy catalogueEditable groups, source colours, prices, durations, five automatic workflows and reviewed Greek/English/German names.
OdontogramPermanent dentition, precise surfaces, bridges and removable prosthetics, plus planned/completed clinical overlays.
Treatment planningAlternative plans, internal discounts, patient-facing A4 PDFs, consent records and completion into treatment history.
Periodontal chartSix-site rapid entry, PD/GM/CAL and clinical markers, lightweight graphs, patient PDF and blank handwriting form.
Calendar workflowPatient search, contact details, patient-file link and per-user Google Calendar synchronization.
Patient recordResponsive contact/demographic data and immutable, dated medical-history revisions.

Suggested feedback pass

  1. Add a filling and crown to a Demo patient; compare planned and completed overlays.
  2. Create two plan alternatives, add a discount and preview another PDF language.
  3. Enter consecutive pocket depths without the mouse and print both periodontal PDF modes.
  4. On a backed-up clinic only, select a patient while making an appointment and test Google sync after OAuth setup.

Important beta notes

  • Demo uses fake, disposable data and includes the 279-treatment demonstration catalogue.
  • A real clinic keeps its own catalogue; this practice's list is never inserted automatically.
  • Treatment plans remain local to this device in this beta. Completing an item creates the clinical event, never a payment.
  • Periodontal charting currently covers permanent teeth; voice entry is reserved but not enabled.
